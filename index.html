Delete Your PartsWallah Account

If you would like to delete your PartsWallah account and associated personal data, please contact us using the account email address.

Important: Account deletion is permanent and cannot be undone.

How to Request Account Deletion

Send an email from the email address associated with your PartsWallah account requesting account deletion.

Request Account Deletion

What Will Be Deleted

When your account is deleted, we will delete or remove information associated with your account, where applicable, including:

  • Your account information
  • Your profile information
  • Your saved account data
  • Other personal information associated with your account

Data That May Be Retained

Certain information may need to be retained where required by law, for fraud prevention, dispute resolution, security, or legitimate business purposes. Any retained information will be handled in accordance with applicable privacy requirements.

Processing Time

Account deletion requests will be reviewed and processed within a reasonable period after the request is received.
